08 / Reflection

My Reflection

I am Mark John Espinosa, a simple boy who wants to be successful in life and achieve my goals, because I want to help my family in the future. We are poor, and that's why I push myself to learn more. My parents also support my goals because they want me to succeed.

Right now, though, I'm still far from reaching my goals. I'm just a student learning programming at Mati National Comprehensive High School, currently in Grade 12 - Marigold. I thought it would be easy, but it turned out to be really hard. Still, I have a dream, and that's why I need to keep learning. This year, I must become an independent learner.

I want to help my family, especially my mother — I want to help her build a house and buy land for our family. For my father, I want to help him buy a car someday and give both of them a great life. And someday, I also hope to find the girl of my dreams.
