Classroom Rules

Our Classroom Values

We believe in mutual respect and creating a safe space for everyone to learn and thrive.

  • Respect: Treat others with kindness and consideration
  • Participation: Engage actively in all classroom activities
  • Honesty: Always do your own work and be truthful
  • Responsibility: Complete assignments on time and come prepared
  • Collaboration: Work together and support your classmates

Consequence System

We follow a progressive approach to help students learn from mistakes.

  1. First reminder - Verbal warning
  2. Second incident - Written note home
  3. Third incident - Parent meeting
  4. Fourth incident - Administrative referral
